VULMS Tools Privacy Policy

Effective date 13 October 2026 · Applies to VULMS Tools for Google Chrome, version 2.2.0 and later · Developer: vulmstools

This policy explains what information the VULMS Tools browser extension (the "extension") reads, stores and sends, why it does so, and what control you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean the developer of the extension. "You" means the person who installs and uses it.

VULMS Tools is an independent product. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with the Virtual University of Pakistan (VU), DigiSkills, Cisco or Cisco Networking Academy, Anthropic, OpenAI or Google.

Appendix: storage reference

1. Summary

  • We collect nothing. We run no server, have no user accounts and receive no data from the extension. It contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • Your data stays on your device, in Chrome's extension storage, except in the cases listed in section 4. Each of those happens only on the websites the extension supports, and almost all of them happen only when you press a button.
  • AI help is optional and off by default. If you turn it on and press Solve with AI, the quiz question you chose is sent straight from your browser to the AI provider you selected (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), using an API key that you supply.
  • The extension works only on vu.edu.pk, digiskills.pk and netacad.com, plus the three AI provider addresses. It cannot read or change any other website.
  • Some features act on your learning platforms on your behalf. These include requesting your own VULMS pages for the Reminders page, recording lecture watch time with your university, and stopping a quiz page from seeing when you switch tabs. Section 4 and section 5 describe each one plainly, so you can decide whether to keep it switched on.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information, and we never use it for advertising, profiling or decisions about credit or lending.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, store or have access to any of the following. Where the extension handles one of these items on your device, the data stays on your device unless section 4 says otherwise:

  • your name, email address, phone number or other contact details;
  • your browsing history, or any information about websites other than the three supported learning platforms;
  • your university marks, quiz answers, assignments, fees or other academic or financial records;
  • your login credentials or API keys;
  • your IP address, device identifiers, location or usage statistics.

4. Information that leaves your device

4.1 AI providers (optional, off by default)

The Solve with AI feature is off until you turn it on in Settings and enter an API key that you obtained from the provider yourself.

Destination When
api.anthropic.com Only when you press Solve with AI or Test key with Anthropic selected
api.openai.com Only when you press Solve with AI or Test key with OpenAI selected
generativelanguage.googleapis.com Only when you press Solve with AI or Test key with Google selected

What is sent when you press Solve with AI:

  • a fixed instruction, which is the same text every time;
  • the quiz question or questions you chose and their answer options, exactly as they appear on the page;
  • the model you selected;
  • your API key, in the request header, as the credential for your own account with that provider.

The request does not include your name, student ID, LMS login, cookies, the page address, your course list, your marks, your other tabs or anything else from the page. Test key sends a minimal request with no question in it, such as a one-word prompt or a request for the provider's list of models, only to check that the key works.

Requests go directly from your browser to the one provider you selected. They do not pass through any server of ours. The provider bills any usage to your account. The answer is shown to you and is not kept by the extension.

Once your request reaches the provider, the provider's own privacy policy and terms, and your agreement with them, govern it. Providers may keep API requests for a period. We cannot see, control or delete anything you send to them.

4.2 Requests to your learning platform, in your own session

These requests go only to the platform you are signed in to. They use your existing session, the same way your own clicks would. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Reminders page (VU LMS). The extension requests your To Do Calendar, Account Book, Lecture Schedule, Notice Board and Home page from vulms.vu.edu.pk. It requests Home only when you are not already on it. On Home, it reads the page in front of you. A refresh happens only after something you do: loading a VULMS page, opening the Reminders page or pressing Refresh. It never fetches on a timer, in the background or from a quiz page.
  • Semester-wise grades (VU LMS). When you open your grade book and a course's credit hours are not yet known, the extension requests your My Study Scheme page once. It does not request the page again until the browser restarts.

4.3 Records the extension writes to your university's systems

The Lecture bypass feature (switched on by default; you can switch it off) changes records that your university keeps about you. It never runs on its own:

  • On a lecture page that has its own completion control, one press of the extension's button marks that lecture complete through the page's own control.
  • Before it records watch time (described below), it shows a preview of the exact values it will send and waits for a second press.
  • For a whole course, it first lists every lecture it will change and asks you to confirm. It then moves through those lectures one page at a time.

Watch-time records contain the following:

  • VU LMS. It submits a lecture watch-time record through the lecture page's own progress-logging function. The record contains your student ID, the course code, semester, lesson number, content ID, video ID, whether the item is a video, and a number of seconds watched. The extension sets that number itself (600 seconds for a video tab, 8 seconds for a reading tab). It is not the time you actually spent on the lecture.
  • DigiSkills. It submits the same kind of progress request that the DigiSkills player sends, including the page address, the video identifiers and a time spent. The time spent is two-fifths of the lecture's length (150 seconds if the length cannot be read), not the time you actually spent.

These records go only to the platform concerned, never to us. They cannot be undone from the extension. Whether using this feature is allowed under your institution's academic rules is your responsibility.

4.4 Chrome sync

Your preferences in Chrome sync storage travel between your own devices through Google's Chrome sync, the same way your bookmarks do, if you have sync turned on. We cannot see them. API keys, saved logins, grades and Reminders data are never synced. To stop syncing preferences, turn off extension sync in Chrome's settings.

4.5 Files you choose to save

  • Screenshots and PDFs of a quiz question or result are created inside your browser and saved to your Downloads folder. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Backup (Settings → Backup) saves a file to your computer that contains your preferences, your feature switches and the reminders you wrote. The reminders part covers every student who has used the extension on this Chrome profile. Your API keys are included only if you tick that option, and the file name then says so. If you include them, keep the file private. Saved vault logins, grades and Reminders data are never included. Restore reads only a backup that you choose or paste in.

4.6 Notifications

If you turn on deadline alerts, Chrome asks for your permission first. The alerts are Chrome notifications, built from what the Reminders page last read. They appear on your screen and in your system's notification centre, and they are not sent anywhere else.

5. Changes the extension makes to pages

So that you understand the extension's full technical scope, here is everything it changes on the supported pages. None of these changes sends data anywhere unless section 4 says so.

  • Appearance: recolours VU LMS in the theme you chose (Light, Dark, Pookie or Spider), adds a loading bar while pages load, highlights the current-lesson link, and adds the extension's buttons and panels to the page.
  • Navigation: Quick jump and Resume lesson press the page's own links for you. The Reminders page adds an entry to the VULMS sidebar.
  • Copy and paste: Assignment copy-paste re-enables copy, paste and text selection that the page blocks in the assignment solution editor.
  • Quiz firewall bypass (switched on by default; you can switch it off): on VU quiz question pages, the extension stops the quiz page from detecting that its tab has lost focus or its window has been hidden. This changes what the quiz page can observe and report about your attempt. It stores nothing and sends nothing.
  • Accounts vault: fills the sign-in form with an account you pick. It never presses Sign In for you.
  • Lecture bypass: see section 4.3.

To run some of these, the extension runs a fixed set of functions that are bundled inside the extension package in the page itself. It never downloads or runs code from the internet, and it treats AI answers as data, never as code.

7. Security

We have designed the extension to keep your data private, but please understand the limits of each protection:

  • Extension storage is private to this extension. Websites, including VU LMS, and other extensions cannot read it.
  • API keys are stored without encryption. Only the extension's background process uses them, and only to send a request to the provider they belong to. However, someone with access to your computer and your signed-in Chrome profile could read them, as they could your saved passwords. On a shared computer, use a key with a spending limit, or do not save one.
  • Vault logins are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, but the encryption key is kept in the same storage. This protects the contents against casual reading, for example in a copied browser profile or on a shared PC. It does not protect them against someone who can run code inside the extension or who has full access to your Chrome profile.
  • The student ID hash is not anonymisation. VU student IDs follow a short, predictable format, so someone with access to your storage could recover an ID by trying likely values. The hash keeps students' reminders apart; it is not a privacy guarantee.
  • Backup files may contain your API keys if you choose to include them. Store them as carefully as you would a password.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Requests to AI providers and to your learning platforms use the encrypted (HTTPS) connections that those services provide.

8. Data retention and deletion

All data the extension stores stays until you delete it or remove the extension. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete.

  • Remove the extension to delete everything it stored on this computer, including API keys, vault logins, grades and Reminders data.
  • Settings → Backup → Clear saved study data deletes what was read from VULMS: grades, credit hours, the Reminders page's saved readings, which announcements you opened, and the alert history. Reminders you wrote yourself stay until you delete them on the Reminders page or remove the extension.
  • Settings → AI provider: remove a saved key to delete only that key.
  • Accounts vault: delete each account in the vault panel, or press Reset vault to empty it at once.
  • Synced preferences are removed when you remove the extension while signed in to the Chrome profile that holds them.
  • Session data is cleared automatically when the browser closes.
  • Data already sent to an AI provider can only be deleted through that provider. Records written to VU or DigiSkills (section 4.3) can only be changed by that platform.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Every feature can be switched on or off in the toolbar popup or in Settings. Switching a feature off stops it in open tabs as well.
  • The AI feature, deadline alerts and notifications stay off unless you turn them on.
  • You can see and remove everything the extension stores by using the controls in section 8.

Depending on where you live, data-protection laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not collect or receive any personal data, we hold nothing to provide, correct or delete. You can exercise these rights over data on your device yourself, using the controls above. For data you send to an AI provider, contact that provider. For records held by your university or DigiSkills, contact that institution.

10. Sharing, sale and the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone. The only transfers are the ones you start yourself and that section 4 describes: a question sent to the AI provider you chose, and requests to the learning platform you are signed in to.

The extension's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements:

  • information is used only to provide the features described in this policy, which serve the extension's single purpose of helping students use their learning platforms;
  • information is not transferred to third parties, except to the AI provider you chose, at your request, to provide the AI feature;
  • information is not used for advertising, and is not sold;
  • information is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending; and
  • no person reads your information, because none of it reaches us.

11. Third-party services and links

  • AI providers. If you use Solve with AI, your request is handled under the privacy policy of the provider you chose: Anthropic, OpenAI or Google.
  • Learning platforms. VU, DigiSkills and Cisco Networking Academy process your activity on their sites under their own policies, including any records the extension writes for you (section 4.3).
  • Links. The extension contains links to the AI providers' key pages and to vulmstools.com. These open in a new tab. Those websites are not part of the extension and have their own privacy policies.

12. Children's privacy

The extension is designed for university and skills-programme students. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

13. International users

The extension runs on your device wherever you are. We do not transfer your data anywhere. If you use the AI feature, your question goes to the provider you chose, which may process it in other countries under its own policy.

14. Changes to this policy

When the extension's handling of data changes, this policy is updated before the change ships, and the effective date at the top changes. Any new place that the extension sends data will be listed here before it ships. The policy for the version you have installed is always available inside the extension, at Settings → About → Privacy. The current version is also published at https://extension.vulmstools.com/privacy.html.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, or want to report behaviour that does not match it, please use the support contact on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
