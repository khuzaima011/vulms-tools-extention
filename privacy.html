VULMS Tools Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 | Applies to version 5.1.1 and later | Developer: vulmstools

VULMS Tools is a Chrome extension for students of the Virtual University of Pakistan. It works only on VU LMS (vulms.vu.edu.pk and other vu.edu.pk pages), DigiSkills (digiskills.pk) and Cisco NetAcad (netacad.com). This policy explains what user data the extension collects, how it uses that data, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Quick summary:
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads and no sign-up. We never sell your data.
  • Almost everything the extension keeps stays in your own Chrome browser.
  • The only data sent to our server is the quiz question you ask VULMS AI to solve, and only when you press Solve with AI.

1. Data we collect

The extension handles the following data, only on the supported sites and only to run the features you use:

  • Your own VU LMS pages. While you are signed in, the extension reads your course list, grade book results and credit hours, deadlines (assignments, quizzes, GDBs, practicals and fee due dates), lecture schedule, notices and the alerts on the VU LMS Home page. It reads them in your own session when you open those pages, open the Reminders page or press Refresh.
  • Your student ID. Read from the page to keep each student's reminders apart on a shared computer. It is stored only as a one-way SHA-256 hash, never as the ID itself.
  • Saved logins. The VU student IDs and passwords you choose to save in the Accounts vault.
  • Quiz and discussion questions. The text of a question and its answer options, when you press Copy, Screenshot, Save as PDF or Solve with AI.
  • Lecture details. The lesson list and video length on a lecture page, when you use Lecture bypass.
  • Your own entries. Reminders you write yourself and your settings (LMS theme, layout, which features are on, alert preferences).
  • Connection details. When you press Solve with AI, our server receives, like any website, the IP address the request comes from and your browser type.

The extension checks the address of the open tab only to see whether it is a supported site. It never stores or sends that address. We do not collect your name, email address, payment details, keystrokes, or your browsing on any other website.

2. How we use the data

Data is used only to provide the features you see in the extension, and for nothing else:

  • Grades and credit hours show your semester-wise grades, SGPA, CGPA and grade trend.
  • Deadlines, schedule, notices, alerts and your own reminders fill the Reminders page and, if you allow notifications, the Chrome alerts before a deadline.
  • Saved logins fill the VU sign-in page when you press Fill. The extension never presses Sign In for you.
  • Questions are copied to your clipboard, saved as an image or PDF, or sent to VULMS AI for a suggested answer, depending on the button you press.
  • Lecture details are used when you press the Lecture bypass button and confirm. The extension then sends VU LMS or DigiSkills the same watch-time record the site's own video player sends, with a fixed watch time. This changes your lecture progress on that site, and the extension cannot undo it.
  • IP address and browser type are used by our server only to keep VULMS AI working and to stop abuse.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, credit checks or lending, and no person reads it.

3. How the data is stored

  • On your device. Grades, credit hours, deadline data, your reminders, alert history, the hashed student ID and saved logins are kept in Chrome's extension storage on your computer (chrome.storage.local). Only this extension can read it.
  • Saved logins are encrypted (AES-GCM) before they are stored. The encryption key is kept in the same browser storage, so this protects the stored logins from casual reading but not from someone who can use your Chrome profile. Save logins only on a computer you trust.
  • Settings are kept in chrome.storage.sync. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome copies them to your other devices through your Google account. Settings hold no personal data.
  • A lecture run in progress is kept in chrome.storage.session, which Chrome clears when it closes.
  • Files you save (screenshots, PDFs of your quiz results, settings backups) go to your Downloads folder or the place you choose. Copied text goes to your clipboard.
  • On our server. VULMS AI does not save the questions it receives or the answers it gives. Its web server log keeps the IP address, time and browser type of each request, never the question, and deletes them automatically within 30 days.

Everything sent to VULMS AI travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The extension runs only code that ships inside it and never downloads code to run.

4. Who we share data with

  • We do not sell, rent or trade user data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • VULMS AI (ai.vulmstools.com). When you press Solve with AI, the question and its options go only to VULMS AI, the extension's own AI service, run by the developer on its own server. The AI model runs on that server, and the question is not passed to any other company or AI service. No name, student ID, password, grades or cookies are sent.
  • VU LMS, DigiSkills and Cisco NetAcad. The extension talks to these sites only in your own signed-in session, the way your own clicks would: it reads pages, and sends a lecture record when you confirm Lecture bypass. What those sites keep is covered by their own privacy policies.
  • Google, through Chrome sync, receives your settings only if you have turned on sync in Chrome.
  • We disclose server logs only if the law requires it.

The extension contacts no other website. Links to the VULMS Tools website (vulmstools.com) open it only when you click them; the extension sends the website no data.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it or remove the extension.
  • In the extension's Settings, "Clear saved study data" removes the grades, credit hours and reminders data read from VU LMS, and the alert history. You can delete your own reminders on the Reminders page and each saved login in the Accounts vault.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored in Chrome. Files you saved to your computer stay until you delete them.
  • Server logs are deleted automatically within 30 days. They hold no name or student ID.

6. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Site access to vu.edu.pk, digiskills.pk and netacad.com: to run the extension's features on those sites. It asks for access to no other website.
  • Storage: to keep the data and settings described above on your device.
  • Tabs: to see whether the open tab is a supported site, send keyboard shortcuts to it and open the extension's pages.
  • Scripting: to apply your LMS theme before a page appears and to run the extension's own built-in tools on the supported sites.
  • Context menus: to add the extension's tools to the right-click menu on the supported sites.
  • Downloads: to save a screenshot or PDF you ask for.
  • Clipboard write: to copy a question when you press Copy.
  • Offscreen: to build the PDF of your quiz result.
  • Alarms: to time the next deadline alert.
  • Notifications (optional, asked only when you turn on deadline alerts): to show those alerts.

7. Children

The extension is made for university students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received by VULMS Tools complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date. After an update that changes what data the extension handles, the extension also shows a short note on VU LMS.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, write to the developer at the contact email shown on the VULMS Tools page in the Chrome Web Store.
