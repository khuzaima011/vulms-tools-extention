VULMS Tools Privacy Policy

Effective date 15 October 2026 · Applies to VULMS Tools for Google Chrome, version 2.3.0 and later · Developer: vulmstools

VULMS Tools (the "extension") is an independent product. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with the Virtual University of Pakistan (VU), DigiSkills, Cisco, Anthropic, OpenAI or Google.

1. In short

  • We collect nothing. We run no server and have no user accounts. The extension contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • Your data stays on your device, in Chrome's extension storage, except in the cases listed in section 3.
  • It works only on vu.edu.pk, digiskills.pk and netacad.com, plus the three AI provider addresses in section 3.1. It cannot read any other website.
  • We never sell or share your information, and never use it for advertising, profiling, credit or lending.

2. What is stored on your device

The extension reads the supported pages you open only to run its features. It keeps the following in Chrome's extension storage, which only this extension can read:

  • your settings: which features are on, your theme, language, appearance, AI provider and model, deadline alert preferences, and where you left its panels;
  • from your own VULMS pages: grade book results and credit hours (for the SGPA/CGPA dashboard), and what the Reminders page last read (deadlines, fee due dates and balances, lecture schedule, notices and announcements);
  • reminders you write yourself, which notices and announcements you have seen, and which alerts were already shown;
  • a SHA-256 hash of your student ID, to keep students apart on a shared computer;
  • your AI provider API keys, if you enter any (not encrypted, see section 6);
  • logins you save in the Accounts vault (a label, the student ID and the password), encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

Language, appearance, AI provider and model, and alert preferences follow your Chrome profile through Chrome sync, if you have it turned on. Everything else stays on this computer. Short-lived working state is cleared when the browser closes. The extension does not store quiz content, AI answers, your submitted work or your browsing history.

3. What leaves your device

Nothing is ever sent to us. Data leaves your device only in these cases.

3.1 AI help (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Solve with AI and enter your own API key, pressing the button sends a fixed instruction, the quiz question you chose with its answer options, the model name and your API key directly to the one provider you selected: api.anthropic.com, api.openai.com or generativelanguage.googleapis.com. It does not send your name, student ID, cookies, the page address or anything else from the page. Test this key sends a minimal request with no question in it. The provider's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.

3.2 Requests to VULMS in your own session

These go only to vulms.vu.edu.pk, using your signed-in session, the same way your own clicks would.

  • Reminders page: reads your To Do Calendar, Account Book, Lecture Schedule, Notice Board and Home page, only after you load a VULMS page, open Reminders or press Refresh. Never on a timer, in the background or from a quiz page.
  • Semester grades: reads your My Study Scheme page once per browser session, when a course's credit hours are not yet known.

3.3 Lecture bypass (on by default)

This feature changes records your university keeps about you. It runs only when you press its button, and before it records watch time it shows the exact values and asks you to confirm.

  • VU LMS: marks a lecture complete through the page's own control, or submits a watch-time record with your student ID, course code, semester, lesson, content and video IDs and a fixed time (600 seconds for a video, 8 for a reading), not the time you actually spent.
  • DigiSkills: submits a progress record with a time of two-fifths of the video's length (150 seconds if the length cannot be read).

These records go only to that platform and cannot be undone from the extension. Whether this feature is allowed under your institution's rules is your responsibility.

3.4 Files and alerts

  • Screenshots, PDFs and backup files are saved to your computer only. A backup includes your API keys only if you tick that option. It never includes vault logins, grades or Reminders data.
  • Deadline alerts are Chrome notifications, shown only on your screen. Chrome asks for your permission first.

4. What the extension changes on pages

On the supported sites it recolours VU LMS in your chosen theme, redraws the VU sign-in and Home pages, and adds a loading bar and its own buttons. On the redrawn sign-in page, what you type goes only into VU's own sign-in form on the same page, and VU signs you in as usual when you press Sign in. It also:

  • re-enables copy and paste in the assignment editor;
  • Quiz firewall bypass (on by default): stops VU quiz pages from detecting that you switched tabs or hid the window. It stores and sends nothing;
  • Accounts vault: fills the sign-in form with an account you pick, but never presses Sign in for you.

All code is bundled in the extension. It never downloads or runs remote code, and it treats AI answers as data, never as code.

6. Security

  • Extension storage is private to this extension. Websites and other extensions cannot read it.
  • API keys are not encrypted. Anyone with access to your Chrome profile could read them. On a shared computer, use a key with a spending limit.
  • Vault logins are encrypted, but the key is stored beside them. This guards against casual reading, not against someone with full access to your profile.
  • The student ID hash can be guessed back, because VU IDs follow a short format. It keeps students apart; it does not anonymise them.
  • Keep a backup file private if it includes your API keys.

7. Deleting your data

  • Remove the extension to delete everything it stored.
  • Settings → Backup settings → Clear saved study data deletes grades, credit hours and Reminders data. Reminders you wrote stay until you delete them.
  • Remove an API key in Settings → AI provider, or all vault logins with Reset vault.
  • Data sent to an AI provider, or records written to VU or DigiSkills, can only be deleted by that provider or platform.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.

8. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The extension's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information is used only to provide the features above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, credit or lending, and not transferred to anyone except the AI provider you chose, when you ask. No person reads your data, because none of it reaches us.

9. Other

  • Third parties: AI requests are covered by the provider's own policy (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google). VU, DigiSkills and Cisco handle your activity on their sites under their own policies. Links to other websites open in a new tab.
  • Children: the extension is for university and skills-programme students and is not directed at children under 13.
  • Changes: this policy is updated before any change in how the extension handles data ships, and the effective date changes with it. It is available in the extension at Settings → About → Privacy policy and at https://extension.vulmstools.com/privacy.html.
  • Contact: use the support contact on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
