VULMS Tools Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 18 October 2026 | Developer: vulmstools

Quick Summary:
  • We collect zero personal data. No analytics, tracking, or ads.
  • Your data stays on your device inside Chrome storage.
  • Works only on supported sites (VU LMS, DigiSkills, NetAcad).

1. Data Stored on Your Device

All settings, themes, GPA records, reminders, and saved account logins are stored locally in Chrome's extension storage and encrypted on your device.

2. External Connections

  • Supported learning sites (vulms.vu.edu.pk, vu.edu.pk, digiskills.pk, netacad.com): Reads your signed-in session pages only when you use features like Reminders or Lecture Watch.
  • AI Helper (Optional): Sends the question and its options only to VULMS AI, the extension's own hosted server at 150.230.148.215, and only when you press Solve. No account or API key is needed.

The extension contacts no other website.

3. Permissions

Permissions (Storage, Tabs, Scripting, Notifications) are used strictly to run extension features locally on supported websites.

4. How to Delete Your Data

Uninstalling the extension instantly removes all stored data. You can also clear study data or saved logins anytime from Extension Settings.
